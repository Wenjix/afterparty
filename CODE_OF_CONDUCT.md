# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in Afterparty to be a respectful, constructive, and
harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of background, identity,
experience, or ability.

## Expected behavior

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- using welcoming and inclusive language;
- respecting differing viewpoints and experiences;
- accepting constructive criticism gracefully;
- focusing on the project and its users; and
- showing empathy toward other participants.

## Unacceptable behavior

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, personal attacks, trolling,
publishing private information without permission, sexualized or discriminatory
conduct, and any other behavior that would reasonably make participation unsafe
or unwelcome.

## Scope and enforcement

This policy applies in project repositories, issues, pull requests, releases,
and project-related public or private spaces. The maintainer may remove, edit,
or reject comments, commits, issues, code, or other contributions that do not
meet this standard, and may restrict participation when necessary.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to [@Wenjix](https://github.com/Wenjix)
through the contact method on the maintainer profile. Do not use a public issue
for a sensitive report. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical.

This policy is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/),
version 2.0.
